Asset optimization: the embedded 2048×2048 texture was converted to JPEG quality 90 and equivalent vertices/index storage were optimized using glTF-Transform. The optimized GLB is 1,835,780 bytes, requires no Draco/Meshopt decoder, and preserves embedded attribution. Presentation changes: normalized scale, centering, studio environment and lighting. The original asset is a single merged mesh with baked color texture and no animation. It is used as a fixed inspirational 360-degree product view, not as the customer's ingredient-configurable burger.

Visible source, author, license and modification credits also appear in the 360-degree dialog.
